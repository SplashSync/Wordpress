WordPress powers more than 40% of the web, from hobby blogs to the largest news sites online.
Beautiful designs, powerful features, and the freedom to build anything you want: WordPress is
free and priceless at the same time.

The Splash plugin gives access to your **posts** and **pages**. With WooCommerce active, it
also covers your **customers**, **addresses**, **products**, **orders** and **invoices**.

### Share your key e-commerce data across all your apps

This plugin was designed to share your financial information with your whole organization.
Your WooCommerce shop becomes one of your *sales channels*: export and synchronize your sales
with Splash, and read them all from a single place.

### Publish your posts across all your websites

If you run several blogs or websites, this plugin brings publishing to the highest level of
automation: write and update your posts from a single point, Splash takes care of the rest.

Using the flexibility of the Splash ecosystem, you decide where new posts are published, which
ones receive updates, and what information travels.

For instance, imagine your company runs two blogs, one for internal communication, one for
external publications. With Splash you can:

- push every new post of the external website to the internal blog;
- prevent new posts of the internal blog from reaching the external one;
- archive each revision of your contents on our servers.

### Synchronize your product stocks

Stocks are critical for your business. Avoid mistakes by keeping them aligned across all your
applications, automatically.

### Merge all your customers data

Once your modules are connected, use the Linker to identify and merge all your customer
profiles into a single Splash entity. Every matching piece of information is then shared and
kept up to date everywhere, from your CRM to your e-commerce.

### Consolidate & simplify your financial analytics

Orders and invoices are exported from WooCommerce to any other application you connect — and
the other way around. Your financial analytics gets easier, with no effort.
